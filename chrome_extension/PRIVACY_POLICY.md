# Sara SOP Capture Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

## Introduction

Sara SOP Capture helps you capture screenshots of browser tabs and organize them into step-by-step standard operating procedures (SOPs). You can use the extension without creating an account.

## Information We Collect

Sara SOP Capture does not transmit information to its developer. Screenshots captured with the extension and images you choose from your device, along with image names, are stored in IndexedDB in your browser. Description groups are stored in the browser's local storage. The extension accesses the active tab when you invoke its preview or capture features.

## How We Use Information

Information stored in the browser is used only to display, edit, organize, save, and copy your SOP content. If you choose **Copy for Document**, the extension places the selected SOP content on your device's clipboard. If you choose a folder and save images, the extension writes image files to that folder at your direction. Folder selection and file saving are optional.

## Data Storage and Security

Content remains in browser storage on your device until you delete it or clear the extension's browser data. Files you save are stored in the folder you selected. The extension does not upload or synchronize your content. The security of your browser profile and selected folder depends on your device and operating system; anyone with access to them may be able to access your files.

## Third-Party Services

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, remote servers, or third-party data processing services.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the extension changes. The latest version and its revision date will be published with the extension.
